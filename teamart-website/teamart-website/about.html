Passion for tea, run like a trading house.

TEAMART LIMITED is a Mombasa-based tea buyer and exporter. We source, blend, evaluate and ship Kenyan Black CTC and Orthodox teas to buyers around the world.

Who we are

TEAMART LIMITED was incorporated in December 2021 as a private limited company in Kenya. We hold the licence of a tea buyer and exporter and work under the supervision of the Tea Board of Kenya, with an active tax compliance record.

We balance high-volume Black CTC for mass-market buyers with premium Orthodox tea for specialty retail, so our customers get a steady supply and our business stays resilient.

How we work

Two ways to source

We buy cataloged lots at the Mombasa Electronic Auction run by EATTA, settling within the 9-day prompt payment rule. We also hold direct supply contracts with independent and KTDA-aligned factories for single-origin batches.

Traceable by design

Every lot is verified back to a factory certified under the Kenya Tea Mark of Origin. Consignments clear the Kenya TradeNet Single Window with KEPHIS phytosanitary, KEBS quality and Tea Board release permits.

Stored and shipped from Mombasa

Tea is held in a Tea Board registered warehouse with moisture-controlled storage and pest-free zoning approved by KEPHIS inspectors. We handle domestic transport, customs and container stuffing.

Packed for your market

Bulk lots go out in multi-wall paper sacks. For retail buyers we pack eco-friendly pyramid bags and branded canisters under 10 kg, using vacuum sealing with nitrogen flush.

Terms buyers can plan around

FOB Mombasa

We price and execute every sale FOB Mombasa. Ocean freight and transit insurance pass to the buyer once the cargo crosses the ship's rail.

Secure payment

Export shipments are paid by irrevocable letter of credit at sight or by advance telegraphic transfer. We keep KES and USD accounts.

Local team

Our plan supports 12 direct jobs across management, operations and quality inspection, with training in digital quality evaluation, auction bidding and phytosanitary compliance.
