Our tea grades

Four Black CTC grades for volume buyers and two Orthodox grades for premium leaf markets. Dots on each card show relative particle size.

Black CTC (Crush, Tear, Curl)

Bright infusions, strong colour and a brisk flavour. Supplied in multi-wall paper sacks over 10 kg.

BP1

Broken Pekoe 1

The largest of our CTC granules. It gives a bright, well-coloured cup with a full, brisk body.

Cup
Bright, brisk, full-bodied
Best for
Blends, household packs, tea bags
Enquire about BP1

PF1

Pekoe Fanning 1

Smaller, dense granules that infuse quickly into a strong, coloury liquor. A dependable base for packers.

Cup
Strong, coloury, quick infusion
Best for
Tea bags, mass-market packing
Enquire about PF1

PD

Pekoe Dust

Fine particles that brew fast and deep. Good where strength and speed matter more than leaf appearance.

Cup
Deep colour, very quick brew
Best for
Tea bags, vending, chai blends
Enquire about PD

D1

Dust 1

The finest of our CTC grades, with the fastest infusion and the strongest liquor.

Cup
Very strong, fastest infusion
Best for
Strong tea bags, industrial use
Enquire about D1

Orthodox (whole and broken leaf)

Traditionally processed leaf that keeps delicate aroma, fruit notes and a complex liquor. Available in bulk or in retail packs under 10 kg.

TGFOP

Tippy Golden Flowery Orange Pekoe

Whole leaf with golden tips from the youngest buds. Expect a fragrant aroma, fruit notes and a clear, bright liquor.

Cup
Delicate, aromatic, fruity
Best for
Premium loose leaf, gift canisters
Enquire about TGFOP

TGBOP

Tippy Golden Broken Orange Pekoe

Broken leaf with golden tips. It brews faster and fuller than whole leaf while keeping its aromatic character.

Cup
Aromatic, fuller body
Best for
Pyramid bags, specialty blends
Enquire about TGBOP
