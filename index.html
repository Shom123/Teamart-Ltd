From factory to ship's rail

  1. Source

    We buy cataloged lots at the Mombasa Electronic Auction and under direct contracts with Kenyan factories.

  2. Inspect

    Every lot traces back to a factory certified under the Kenya Tea Mark of Origin, then we evaluate quality.

  3. Blend and pack

    Bulk sacks or retail packs, blended and sealed to your specification.

  4. Clear and ship

    We handle KEPHIS, KEBS and Tea Board clearances and load your container at Mombasa.
